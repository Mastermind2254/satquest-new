CLASSIFIED MISSION BRIEFING

OPERATION ARCTIC LIFELINE

THE BACKGROUND

Global climate research heavily relies on data gathered from the most extreme environments on Earth. Currently, an international team of scientists has established a crucial, year-round research outpost deep within the Arctic Circle. This region experiences harsh environmental conditions, prolonged periods of darkness, and absolute geographical isolation.

THE CRISIS

The research team is facing a critical logistical emergency. Their polar location is a severe "dead zone" for standard global networks. Because most conventional space infrastructure orbits directly over the equator, the physical curvature of the Earth permanently blocks their signals from reaching the Arctic outpost. Without a stable, high-bandwidth connection, the team is completely cut off, unable to transmit their heavy climate models back to headquarters and, crucially, unable to call for emergency support.

THE DIRECTIVE

Your space agency has been contracted to bridge this geographical gap. You must design and launch a specialized space mission to establish a continuous, reliable link with the isolated researchers. To succeed, your team must analyze the geographical constraints of the target area, select the appropriate onboard technology to process and transmit heavy data loads, and calculate the exact trajectory required to keep the outpost connected to the outside world.
